# Milestone 3 Demo Guide

### 1. Open Live Monitoring
Select a patient and review the latest heart rate, BP, SpO2 and glucose.

### 2. Show normal monitoring
Click **Start live monitoring**. Normal readings are stored and the recent-reading list updates.

### 3. Trigger an alert
Click **Simulate HR 145 bpm**. The system stores the reading and generates a CRITICAL heart-rate alert. The same reading also demonstrates multi-signal threshold evaluation.

### 4. Explain the alert
Point out: severity, observed value, threshold, time, recipient team and acknowledgement state. Click **Acknowledge** to complete the workflow.

### 5. Connect Milestone 2
Open **AI Risk Lab** and use the three seeded patient profiles to demonstrate LOW, MODERATE and HIGH risk levels rather than showing only LOW results.
